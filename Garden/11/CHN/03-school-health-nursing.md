**School nursing** is a type of public health nursing that focuses on the promotion of health and wellness of the pupils/students, teaching and non-teaching personnel of the schools. School nurses also assist young people in making choices for a healthy lifestyle, reduce risk-taking behavior and focus on issues such as prevention of drug and substance abuse, teenage pregnancy, sexually transmitted infection, malnutrition, and communicable and non-communicable diseases.

>[!TIP] Goal of School Nursing
>**To promote and maintain the health of the school populace by providing comprehensive and quality nursing care**. This may be achieved with the following specific objectives:
>1. Provide quality nursing service.
>2. Create awareness for promotive and preventive health among school children, personnel, and administrators through health education.
>3. Encourage the provision of standard functional facilities.
>4. Provide nursing personnel with opportunities for continuing education and training.
>5. Conduct and participate in researches related to nursing care.
>6. Establish/strengthen linkages with government and non-government organizations or agencies for school community health work.

In schools, the **Assessment of Pupils for Public and Private Schools** (R.A. 124) mandated the creation of an assessment procedure for children:
- **Nutritional status**, according to the WHO, can be determined via **height and weight by age** for <5 year old children or **body mass index** for >5 years old.
- **Visual acuity**: Snellen chart
- **Hearing acuity**: Ballpen click test. Position the pen two to three centimeters away from the ear and click it two to three times while occluding the contralateral ear. Repeat the test for both ears and assess for the volume of the clicking.
- **Physical examination**: in the school, assessment is done in order: arms, eyes, nose, mouth, ears, neck and chest, hair, and lower extremities.
	- The neck, chest, and extremities are common areas for rash diseases of children, including measles, chickenpox, etc.
	- The eyes are checked for visual acuity.
	- The nose is checked for hygiene.
	- The mouth is checked for dental condition.
	- The ears are checked for infections and mastoiditis. This carries the risk for affecting the meninges, resulting in meningitis.
	- The hair is checked for lice.
