**Clinic visits** are health appointments at the BHS, RHU, or Lying-in clinics—the first contact facilities. This occurs in phases:
1. **Pre-consultation conference**: identification of the purpose of the visit; the **chief complaint**. This is the most important data to gather in this phase.
2. **Medical examination phase**: performed by doctors, etc.
3. **Nursing intervention phase**
4. **Post-consultation conference**: provision of health teaching to promote health.
	- Health teaching is composed of three parts%% (mn. ICE)%%: Information, Communication, and Education.
	- A criteria is applied for health teaching, in order of importance, are%%( mn. *KSA*):%% Knowledge, Skills, and Attitude

___

# Clinic Visit Activities
1. **Registration**
2. **Waiting Time**
3. **Triaging**
	- Program-based: following DOH programs and protocols (e.g., IMCI) to enable interventions at the primary level.
	- Non-program-based: cases that are beyond the scope of the health center. The primary avenue to take then is to refer to the appropriate entity.
	- Emergency Case-Based: the provision of first aid or immediate intervention prior to referral.
4. **Clinical Evaluation**
5. **Laboratory Exams**: screening tests (e.g., rapid, initial tests; specimen collection)
6. **Dispensing OTC and Rx Drugs according to DOH programs**. This commonly includes%% (mn. CARIPPON)%% cotrimoxazole, amoxicillin, rifampicin, isoniazid, pyrazinamide, paracetamol, oral rehydrating solution (Oresol), nifedipine. These address infection, tuberculosis, fevers, dehydration, and hypertension.
7. **Referral system**: the Comprehensive Two-way Referral System is used in the Philippines
8. **Health Education**
