# Millennium Development Goals
In September 6, 2000, 191 countries conferenced at UNHQ, NYC for the *Millennium Summit* that established the **Millennium Development Goals** (MDGs). MDGs established eight goals to be met by the year 2015.
1. **Poverty**
2. **Education**
3. **Gender Equality**
4. **Child Mortality**
5. **Maternal Health**
6. **AIDS**, **HIV**, **Malaria**, and other diseases
7. **Environmental Sustainability**
8. **Global Partnership**

# Sustainable Development Goals
In September 25, 2015, 193 countries conferenced at UN at HQ, NYC for the "*Transforming Our World: The 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development*" establishment of the **Sustainable Development Goals** (SDGs, also known as Global Goals). SDGs established seventeen goals to be met by the year 2030.

The 17 SDGs carry on the work begun by the Millennium Development Goals (MDGs), which galvanized a global campaign from 2000-2015 to end poverty in its various dimensions. Yet while the MDGs only applied to developing countries, the SDGs will apply universally to all UN member states, and are considerably more comprehensive and ambitious than the MDGs. It tackles the following:
1. **Poverty**
2. **Hunger**: the SDG being met by nutrition programs and legislations such as R.A. 8976, R.A. 11148, etc.
3. **Health and Wellbeing**: the SDG being met by health-related interventions, e.g., immunizations
4. **Education**
5. **Gender Equality**
6. **Clean Water and Sanitation**: the Global Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) program
7. **Clean Energy**
8. **Decent Work and Economic Growth** may relate to the current global demand for healthcare workers.
9. **Industry, Innovation, Infrastructure**
10. **Reduced Inequalities**, tackled by R.A. 11223
11. **Sustainable cities**
12. **Responsible consumption**: hospital-based consumption of resources produce large amounts of biomedical waste.
13. **Climate Action**: global warming, emergent diseases, etc.
14. **Life below Water**
15. **Life on Land**: zoonotic diseases
16. **Peace and Justice**: patient safety, bioethical principles, and legal liabilities outlined in R.A. 9173.
17. **Partnership for Goals**: implementations of health programs are always *multisectoral*; *collaborative*. The organizations involved in the implementation of these programs are called "*stakeholders*".
