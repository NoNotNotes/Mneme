> [!EXAMPLE] Situation 1
> The Philippine Nursing Act of 2002 regulates the practice of nursing in the Philippines, setting the standards for education, licensure, and professional practice. It ensures that nurses provide safe, ethical, and competent care while protecting the public through proper regulation.

1. The Philippine Nursing Act of 2002 is an act providing for a more responsive nursing profession. It is known as which Republic Act?
<ol class="mcq-options">
	<li>R.A. 7164</li>
	<li>R.A. 2382</li>
	<li>R.A. 9173</li>
	<li>R.A. 9165</li>
</ol>

>[!FAQ]- Rationalization
>C. **R.A. 9173**. R.A. 7164 is the previous nursing act of 1991. R.A. 2832 is the medical act of 1959. R.A. 9165 is the comprehensive dangerous drug acts of 2022.
>
>Significant differences between the previous and current nursing act:
>- The Board of Nursing used to be composed of 1 chairperson and 4 members. Currently, it is composed to 1 chairperson and 6 members.
>- Only the upper 40% of the graduating class was allowed to take the board exam. Currently, it is open to all.
>- Previously, only three attempts were allowed for the board exam. Currently, there is no limit.

> [!EXAMPLE] Situation 6
>
> In our everyday lives, the “rightness” or “wrongness” of a situation or how to act can seem very clear. However, very often in healthcare, nurses constantly face the uncertain space known as the “gray area”. 26. A nurse who gives out information without authorization from the patient or from the legally responsible guardian can be held liable for violating which ethical principle?
<ol class="mcq-options">
	<li>Veracity</li>
	<li>Autonomy</li>
	<li>Nonmaleficence</li>
	<li>Beneficence</li>
</ol>

>[!FAQ]- Rationalization
>B. **Autonomy**. Autonomy is the most important; related to self-determination. The nurse violated this principle by deciding in place of the patient. Beneficence is "*doing good*". Nonmaleficence is "*avoiding harm*". Veracity is *truth-telling*. Confidentiality is *honoring privacy*. Fidelity is *loyalty to one's oath; promise-keeping*. Solidarity is *unity*. Stewardship is *care-taking*.

	<li>Justice</li>
</ol>

>C. **Nonmaleficence**. The patient avoids inducing pain with this action.
