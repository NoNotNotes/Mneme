This section compiles as many sample questions I could get my hands on for the PNLE (and other standardized examinations like the RENR and NCLEX). Many of these are from pages e.g. **NurseLabs** and from documents uploaded to file-hosting pages such as **Scribd**, **Studocu** and others.

>[!WARNING] Disclaimers
>1. **Mistakes**. I spent weeks collecting, formatting, parsing, and re-formatting dozens of documents for these collections. Many documents were PDFs, which I converted via third-party transcoders which utilize OCRs. Missing letters, misread characters, mismatched choices or answers, etc. are expected to be present.
>2. **I did not make these questions**. Their content and answer are all based on the documents used for this collection. Always stay open to the possibility of the answer key being wrong. If you have strong inkling that an answer is wrong, refer to textbooks or other reliable sources to determine the right answer (*or just ignore it!*). Many questions do not have rationalizations, and potentially may not even require them.
>3. **Questions may be miscategorized**. These questions are broadly categorized between domains they were originally categorized under, but these are not entirely rigid categorizations.
